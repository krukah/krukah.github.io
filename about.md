I am Kelechi Ukah, founder and CTO at [Novig](https://novig.com), a
peer-to-peer sports exchange. At night I build
[Robopoker](https://robopoker.io), a no-limit hold'em solver, and
[Navier](https://github.com/krukah/navier), a Hyperliquid client. Both in
Rust.

Before those: physics and mathematics at Harvard on a Herchel Smith
fellowship, then Run III work on the ATLAS experiment at CERN — calibrating
New Small Wheel detectors and writing combinatoric algorithms for jet
reconstruction. Then quantitative trading at Jane Street, which is where the
exchange habit started. Same job every time — decide what a thing is worth
before anyone tells you.

Away from a terminal: jazz piano, a short film I wrote and directed, a scene
I led in Harvard Eleganza MMXXII, and several years teaching math, physics,
statistics, and informatics.

This site is written by hand and published from
[github.com/krukah/krukah.github.io](https://github.com/krukah/krukah.github.io).
Corrections are welcome by email.
