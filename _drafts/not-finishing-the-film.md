I wrote a short film, directed it, and got about eighty percent of the way
through the edit before I stopped. That was four years ago. The footage still
lives on a drive on my desk.

What I remember is not the shoot, which was fine, but the specific quality of
the stall. Software has a shape when it is unfinished: a test fails, a page
does not render, the thing does not run. An edit has no such signal. It plays
start to finish at every stage of completion. It is always watchable and
never done, and the difference between the two is a judgement you have to
make from nothing.

I have come to think that is the actual skill, and that I did not have it. I
had taste enough to know the cut was not right and no method for finding out
why. In code that gap gets closed by instrumentation. In an edit you close it
by watching the thing two hundred more times, and I ran out of patience
before I ran out of ideas.
